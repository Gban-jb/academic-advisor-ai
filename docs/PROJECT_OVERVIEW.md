# The Advising Place

A simple guide to the concept.

---

## In one sentence

A website that reads a student's transcript and instantly shows them
which classes to take each semester — all the way to graduation.

It's like a **GPS for your degree.**

---

## The problem

Students always ask: *"What do I take next? Will I graduate on time?"*

Getting the answer is hard:

- Advisors are busy.
- The rules are buried in 200-page handbooks.
- One wrong class = a wasted semester.

We make the answer instant and correct.

---

## How a student uses it

1. Land on a friendly welcome page.
2. Pick their school. See its majors, minors, and classes.
3. Upload their transcript (a PDF or photo).
4. Get a clear plan, semester by semester.

---

## How it works (3 simple parts)

**The reader** — an AI reads the uploaded transcript and pulls out the classes.

**The planner** — fixed rules (not AI) build the schedule. It knows which
class needs another first, and balances each semester. We keep this part
rule-based on purpose, so the plan is always reliable.

**The website** — the clean pages the student clicks through.

---

## Our starting point: AAMU first

We build the whole thing for **one school first — Alabama A&M University.**

Doing one school start-to-finish lets us get everything right before we grow:
the experience, the rules, the accurate class list, and the transcript reader.

Where we are with AAMU today:

- Accurate Computer Science class list from the real handbook
- Transcript reading
- The full student experience (welcome → school page → planner)
- A polished design
- Live online

Still to add for AAMU:

- Saving (accounts), so plans don't disappear
- The other majors (only Computer Science works so far)

---

## The plan to grow

Here's the order of the journey:

1. **Finish AAMU fully** — every major, saving, a complete experience.
2. **Train our own model here** — use the real AAMU data to build and
   fine-tune our own transcript-and-advising AI, tuned to how real
   transcripts and handbooks actually look.
3. **Use that proven approach to add more schools** — once it works
   beautifully for AAMU, we repeat the pattern school by school.
4. **Scale up** — many schools, a chatbot for student questions, mobile.

The hard part of growing isn't the website — it's the data. Every school has
different classes and rules, updated every year. Getting AAMU completely
right first, and training our own model on it, is what makes adding the next
schools faster and more accurate.

---

## Our AI approach (the concept)

The idea is to **build and fine-tune our own model**, starting with AAMU.

By training on real AAMU transcripts and the real handbook, the model learns
how actual school documents look — so it reads them accurately, even when
they're messy. Once it's proven here, the same trained approach extends to
new schools.

The planning itself stays rule-based, not AI — a graduation plan should be
reliable, never a guess. AI handles the reading and, later, answering
student questions.

---

## Keeping data safe

Transcripts are private. We encrypt everything, only the student sees their
data, and we follow student-privacy rules. Trust comes first.
